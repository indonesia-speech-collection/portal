Before you start
  • Record in a quiet room (no fan, TV, traffic or people talking).
  • Hold the phone about 20 cm from your mouth. Do not touch or move it while recording.
  • Speak in standard Indonesian, clearly, at a normal speed and normal volume.
  • Read exactly what is written. No extra words, no repeats.
  • Do not record if you have a cold or a hoarse voice.
  • Silence before and after every sentence: press Record and stay quiet until the button turns green and says SPEAK NOW. After the last word, stay quiet again — the recording stops by itself.
  • Use only your registered phone. Your ID works on one phone only.
